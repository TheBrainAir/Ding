Food allergies?

Every dish shows the allergens it contains. Our kitchen handles peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, soy, gluten, milk, eggs, fish, shellfish and molluscs. We do not remove allergens from a dish on request, so please choose a dish that is free from yours. The tofu for the bao is fried in a fryer shared with shellfish. With a severe allergy, please also speak to the manager on duty.
